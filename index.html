BRIDGED HUMANS - BRIDGED

Bridged Humans

In this game you play as two little characters and these characters are connected via a rope you are tasked to sole puzzles while making sure that the rope does not break and you must find creative solutions to finish the levels.

BRIDGED HUMANS -

Developed in 2025 - Platform: PC
The game was a game jam game and it was made in 7 days. The theme of the game jam was connect and i tackled this theme by having two connected characters that are tasked to solve various puzzles in order to escpase rooms. There is a rope between the characters that breaks when you stray too far from each other. The character not being used will follow you automcatically which you quickly learn that its all helpful in some cases
The Game's first level is a bit buggy when it comes to grabbing the wheel so you might need to be creative on that. but the other levels work fine so maybe try those first

Game Mechanics: Moving and Jumping. Switching Characters. Grabbing items and levers. Maintain Rope Tension through Distance
